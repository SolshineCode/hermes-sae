## 2. Behavioral findings

**Same-inference invariant honored.** Metrics are computed on the *actual* layer-23 residual stream captured by the forward hook inside `nla_server.py`'s `model.generate()` call — no replay, no second model (REPORT.md §4).

**Degenerate-loop attractor observed (the headline).**
- Generation norms sit in a narrow band (~48–69 across turns); the model does not roam representation space.
- `consec_cos` (cosine of consecutive generated-token activation vectors) is **sustained and high**: per-turn means 0.455–0.674, with individual pairs reaching **0.93**. Sustained high consec-cos = the trajectory is barely moving through feature space = a low-diversity / near-fixed-point attractor.
- **First hard-loop signal:** turn `cda2c8ef` (Hermes, n=16) shows `loop-frac(cos>0.9)=0.071` — ~7% of consecutive token transitions are near-identical (cos>0.9), the only run with a non-zero loop fraction. This is the empirical onset of the degenerate-loop attractor the capture rig was built to detect.
- Across all 15 runs, `loop-frac>0.9` is otherwise 0.0 — i.e. full degeneration is rare, but the *soft* attractor (high consec-cos without exact repetition) is the norm.

**Prefill vs generation.** Prefill norms (~52–59) are consistently close to gen norms (~52–61); the model enters generation already near the attractor basin rather than drifting into it.

**Hermes vs curl.** No systematic separation in norm/loop metrics between agent-integrated (Hermes) and raw-probe (curl) traffic in this sample — both occupy the same bounded region. The agent's own multi-turn context (`0bed84ea`, n=96) shows the same attractor (consec-cos mean 0.569, max 0.796, loop-frac 0.0), so the behavior is model-intrinsic, not an artifact of the agent harness.

**Open caveat (stated honestly).** These are *raw residual activations*. A trained SAE for Gemma-4-E2B layer 23 has **not yet been wired in**, so we have not yet decomposed the attractor into interpretable feature directions. The capture format is SAE-ready.
